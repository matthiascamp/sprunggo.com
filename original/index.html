We are an Australian new product development company specialising in foot and ankle products. We are introducing Sprung-go, our new children's bounce, walk, run shoe attachment.

Sprung-go is like walking on clouds. Safety features include anti-ankle-roll stability. The footplate only moves vertically upwards and downwards with each step. Our design also allows for a natural heel-to-toe motion during walking, feeling comfortable and safe.

INTRODUCING SPRUNG-GO BOUNCE SHOES

Currently we have manufactured Sprung-go from aluminium making our prototypes its current weight is 570 grams this will be reduced to 310 grams when manufactured using plastic mold injection in material glass filled polymer or similar heavy-duty plastic. Manufactured in plastic will reduce the current tolerances making Sprung-go even better.  It is possible to increase or decrease the spring/cushion effect to suit different body weights. It’s also possible to increase the spring/ bounce lift for children who like more extreme sports.
